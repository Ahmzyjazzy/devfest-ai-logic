# Qorelly Store catalogue

Qorelly Store is an online shop in Ilorin, Nigeria.

## Delivery
- Ilorin: same day for orders placed before 2pm, ₦1,500.
- Other cities in Nigeria: 2–4 working days, ₦3,500.
- Free delivery on orders over ₦50,000.

## Returns and refunds
- Return unused items within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Wellness and health items can be returned only if the seal is unbroken.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3 working days.

## Payment
- Card, bank transfer or pay on delivery (Ilorin only).
