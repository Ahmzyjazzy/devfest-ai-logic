# DevFest Store catalogue

DevFest Store is a pop-up gadget shop at DevFest Ilorin 2026, powered by Qorelly. Orders can be picked up at the booth or delivered anywhere in Nigeria.

## Delivery
- Pick up free at the DevFest Store booth, same day.
- Ilorin: same day for orders placed before 2pm, ₦1,500.
- Other cities in Nigeria: 2–4 working days, ₦3,500.
- Free delivery on orders over ₦50,000.

## Returns and refunds
- Return unused items within 7 days for a full refund.
- Opened earbuds can be returned only if they are faulty.
- Every gadget has a 6-month warranty against faults.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3 working days.

## Payment
- Card, bank transfer, or pay at the booth.
